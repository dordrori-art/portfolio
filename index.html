04 About

I make games that are easy to pick up, and drawings that are hard to put down.

I’m Dor Drori, a game designer, artist and level designer. For the past five years at Pikoya I’ve made browser games where the creative side is mine: I draw all the art, design the levels and shape the UX. Away from the design doc I draw in ink, cut linocuts, paint and animate, and that hands-on craft is why my games look and feel the way they do. I learn fast, I keep up with creative technology, and I’m always looking for the next thing to build. If you’re making a game or a visual world, I’d like to help.
