The game

A pull-the-pin puzzle adventure starring Craby, a little crab on a quest. Each level is a small physics puzzle: pull the golden pins in the right order to open a path, avoid the traps and guide Craby safely to the goal.

Key features

  • Pin-pulling physics puzzles with traps and alternate paths
  • Levels designed to be solvable without hints
  • Free to play in the browser; also on the Microsoft Store
