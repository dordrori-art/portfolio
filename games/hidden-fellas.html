The game

A hand-drawn hidden-object game for the browser. Players search detailed black-and-white miniature worlds (Wild Jungles, an Industrial Center and a futuristic city) for hidden Fellas and items, interacting with the scenery along the way: trimming leaves, tickling tigers, poking UFOs.

Key features

  • Three hand-drawn interactive worlds
  • Click objects in the scene to get hints about hiding places
  • Free to play in the browser, no download
