The game

A relaxing 3D take on mahjong solitaire set in a calm forest clearing. Instead of a flat layout, the tiles form a three-dimensional block that players rotate freely to find free, matching pairs and clear the board before time runs out.

Key features

  • 360° board rotation: matches hide on every face of the 3D layout
  • Levels grow more complex as you progress
  • Free to play in the browser; also on the Microsoft Store
