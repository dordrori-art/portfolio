The game

Classic mahjong solitaire with a travel theme. Players clear levels by matching free tile pairs, and the tile motifs, made specifically for this game, are inspired by famous places around the world.

Key features

  • Custom tile motifs inspired by places around the world
  • Timed levels with on-screen hint and pause controls
  • Free to play in the browser, no download
